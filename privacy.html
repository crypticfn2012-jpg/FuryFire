Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FuryFire handles information when you play. The website or portal hosting the game may separately process information under its own privacy policy.

Information handled by the game

FuryFire does not require an account or ask you to provide your name or email address to play. Settings such as language, audio volume, and visual preferences may be saved in your browser's local storage on your device so they can be remembered next time.

Game portals and platform services

When FuryFire is hosted by a third-party portal such as CrazyGames, that platform may process technical data, use cookies or similar technologies, serve advertisements, measure performance, or provide platform features. Such processing is controlled by the platform and described in its own privacy policy. Please review the privacy information of the website where you play.

Analytics and advertising

The game may use the hosting platform's SDK to report loading and gameplay state or request platform-provided ad placements when enabled. The game itself does not use those SDK calls to ask you for personal information. The platform may process data associated with those features under its own policies.

Retention and choices

Locally saved preferences remain in your browser until you clear the game's site data. You can play without changing optional settings. The hosting platform may have separate controls for cookies, ads, or account-related data.

Children's privacy

FuryFire is not designed to request personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted directly to the game, contact the publisher through the official links published alongside the game.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the game changes. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision. For questions about information processed by a game portal, contact that platform directly.
